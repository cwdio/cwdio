# cwdio

**The file manager that keeps up with your terminal.**

Two folders side by side, a terminal that always knows where you are, and an operation queue you can trust
with a 10 GB move. Built for developers — and for anyone who spends real time moving, sorting and cleaning
up files.

**[⬇ Download for Windows](https://github.com/cwdio/cwdio/releases/latest)** · free while in beta · no
account · [cwdio.com](https://cwdio.com)

## Install

| | |
|---|---|
| **Installer** | Per-user, no administrator prompt, updates itself in the background |
| **Portable `.zip`** | Installs nothing — unzip it, run it, delete the folder when you're done |

**Windows will warn you once.** cwdio is new and not yet code-signed, so SmartScreen shows *"Windows
protected your PC"*. That is not a virus alert — Windows has not seen the file often enough to recognise
it, and every new independent app gets the same treatment. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

Every release publishes `SHA256SUMS.txt` if you would rather verify the download first. A signing
certificate is on the 1.0 list, which removes the prompt for good.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11.

## What it does

**With a terminal**

- Navigate a panel and the terminal follows; type `cd` and the panel follows. Multiple shells, each
  tracking its own pane — including over SSH.
- Search by name instantly, or inside files, skipping what your `.gitignore` already lists. Results are
  real rows: copy, move, rename or delete straight from them.
- Open an SFTP or FTP server and work in it like a local drive, with a real SSH shell on the same
  connection.
- Step into ZIP, 7z, RAR and tar archives like folders — including encrypted ones.

**Without one**

- Rename hundreds of files at once, with the new names previewed before you commit and one Ctrl+Z to undo.
  Photos can take their date from the camera; music from its tags.
- See what is eating your disk as a map where the biggest folders are the biggest blocks — and delete from
  the same screen.
- Find duplicates safely: every copy but one is pre-selected, and they go to the Recycle Bin.
- Compare two folders and sync them, choosing which side wins, with nothing moving until you say so.
- Press Space to look inside anything — photos, video, audio, PDFs, spreadsheets, code.

**The safety net is on by default.** Deleting goes to the Recycle Bin. Overwritten files are kept, not
destroyed. Ctrl+Z undoes the batch — including anything the AI assistant did.

## Privacy

No telemetry. The app does not phone home, and there is no analytics SDK to switch off. Your AI provider
key and your server credentials stay on your machine, encrypted by the OS. Crash reports are opt-in and
scrub your username and home path before they are sent.
